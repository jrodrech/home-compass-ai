# HomeCompass AI - Ubiquitous Language

## Purpose

This document defines the shared vocabulary used throughout the HomeCompass AI platform.

The objective is to ensure that product requirements, domain models, code, database structures, and AI interactions use consistent terminology.

The terms defined here represent business concepts, not merely technical objects.

---

# Core Concepts

## UserAccount

### Definition

A **UserAccount** represents a registered person who has access to the HomeCompass AI platform.

It represents the application identity of a person.

A UserAccount is responsible for:

- Authentication identity
- Account lifecycle
- Access control
- Ownership of personal data
  A UserAccount does not represent the person's real estate needs directly.

### Example

A person creates an account using an email address.

That account may later contain one or more buyer profiles.

---

## BuyerProfile

### Definition

A **BuyerProfile** represents the real estate search context of a user.

It describes the person's:

- Financial reality
- Housing needs
- Lifestyle preferences
- Accessibility requirements
- Personal priorities
  The BuyerProfile is the main input used by the recommendation system.

### Example

A user may have a profile:

> "Looking for a primary residence in Miami, with two bedrooms, affordable monthly payment, good accessibility, and proximity to essential services."

### Important distinction

A UserAccount is the platform identity.

A BuyerProfile represents a business intention.

One UserAccount may eventually have multiple BuyerProfiles.

Example:

```
UserAccount
 |
 +-- Primary Residence Profile
 |
 +-- Investment Property Profile
```

---

## Property

### Definition

A **Property** represents a real estate asset that can be analyzed and evaluated by the system.

A Property contains information required to determine compatibility with buyer needs.

Examples:

- Location
- Price
- Physical characteristics
- Amenities
- Accessibility features
- Financial characteristics
  A Property is not inherently good or bad.

Its value depends on the relationship with a specific BuyerProfile.

---

## Match

### Definition

A **Match** represents the compatibility relationship between a BuyerProfile and a Property.

A Match answers the question:

> "How suitable is this property for this specific buyer?"
> A Match may include:

- Compatibility score
- Positive factors
- Concerns
- Trade-offs
  Example:

```
Property A
+
BuyerProfile B

=
87% Match

Reasons:
- Fits budget
- Meets bedroom requirement
- Close to preferred services

Concerns:
- Higher HOA than desired
```

---

## Recommendation

### Definition

A **Recommendation** represents a ranked suggestion generated by the HomeCompass AI system.

A Recommendation is the result of evaluating multiple properties against a BuyerProfile.

A Recommendation should be explainable.

It should communicate:

- Why the property was selected
- Which buyer needs it satisfies
- Potential limitations
  The system should not only provide a ranking but also support decision making.

---

# AI-Related Concepts

## AI Assistant

### Definition

The AI Assistant is the conversational interface that helps users express their needs naturally.

Its responsibility is:

- Understand human language
- Extract relevant information
- Help create or refine a BuyerProfile
  The AI Assistant does not make final business decisions.

---

## Recommendation Engine

### Definition

The Recommendation Engine is the business capability responsible for evaluating properties against buyer profiles.

It uses:

- Business rules
- Scoring algorithms
- Machine learning models
  The Recommendation Engine produces Matches and Recommendations.

# Architectural Principles Reflected in the Language

## Human Understanding vs Decision Making

The system separates:

```
Human conversation

        |
        v

AI Assistant
(Language understanding)

        |
        v

Structured BuyerProfile

        |
        v

Recommendation Engine

        |
        v

Match / Recommendation
```

The AI helps understand the user.

The recommendation system applies business logic.

- RecommendationHistory

# Evolution Rule

Whenever a new important concept appears:

1. Define the business meaning.
2. Add it to this document.
3. Review existing concepts for consistency.
4. Reflect the decision in the domain model.
   The domain language is a living artifact.
